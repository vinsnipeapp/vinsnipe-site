Common questions

A cross-reference number looks wrong. Use the "report an issue" option on the results screen in the app — it tells us exactly which vehicle and part number to check.

My vehicle isn't in the database. VinSnipe will automatically search online for a match and show you what it finds, clearly marked as unverified until you confirm it against the vehicle.

Where does this data come from? See our Privacy Policy for details on how vehicle lookups work.
